2026-09-15: Paused Flock Fantasy Rewind (fantasy football clips), made
kereks_sports (existing sports card Instagram + TikTok) the ACTIVE
money-maker instead.

The Flock League. The owner could not reach the league directly; the only
confirmation obtained was a DM "yes" from an unnamed team manager, which was
too narrow and unverifiable to authorize clipping the full league's content.

cards, so there is no third-party content-rights question at all.

Flock Fantasy Rewind's work is preserved, not deleted, and can be resumed if
the owner ever confirms real permission. See PROFILE.md Section C-2 and
