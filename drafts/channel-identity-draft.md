# Channel identity - DRAFT (not yet approved)

Niche: short-form fantasy football clips/moments/reactions, sourced initially from
The Flock League ecosystem (pending confirmed clipper permission - see PROFILE.md),
with room to grow into fantasy football content generally.
Audience: sports/fantasy content browsers - casual scrollers who like sports drama,
reactions, and highlight-style moments; may not know the league yet.
Vibe: clean production, high energy.

## Name / handle options (pick one, or ask for a different direction)

1. **Snap Count** - the tension before the ball snaps. Short, punchy, easy to say,
   works for fantasy football broadly (not locked to one league).
2. **Fourth & Forever** - playful twist on "fourth and long." Dramatic, high-stakes
   framing that fits sports-drama/reaction content.
3. **Redzone Rewind** - "redzone" is instantly recognizable to football fans;
   "rewind" signals highlight/replay-style clips.
4. **Waiver Wire** - a real fantasy football term (players picked up off waivers).
   Strong recognition with fantasy players specifically, less so with casual browsers.
5. **Hail Mary** - iconic, dramatic football term. Fits clutch/big-moment energy.

Note: none of these use "Flock" in the name. Until permission is confirmed, a name
that doesn't imply official affiliation with the league is the safer choice, and it
keeps the channel free to grow beyond one source if needed.

## Color palette (draft)

Clean-but-high-energy calls for a dark neutral base with one loud accent color,
rather than a busy multi-color scheme:

- Primary/dark: #12151C  (near-black navy - backgrounds, clean base)
- Accent: #FF4B1F  (hot orange-red - hype moments, callouts, thumbnails)
- Secondary: #F5F5F0  (off-white - text on dark, clean contrast)
- Highlight: #FFD23F  (gold-yellow - stat call-outs, "big play" moments)

## Voice guide (draft)

- Tone: energetic but not screamy; confident, a little cheeky.
- Use: real football/fantasy terms (snap, redzone, waiver, sleeper, bust), short
  punchy sentences, present tense for drama ("he drops it").
- Avoid: corporate filler, forced hype-bro slang, overly long captions.
- Example line: "Waiver wire pickup goes for 3 TDs. You're welcome."
