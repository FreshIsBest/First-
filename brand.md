# Brand: Flock Fantasy Rewind

Approved by the owner. This file is the on-brand source of truth every later
build step (content agent, asset step, posting) reads.

## Name
Chosen: Flock Fantasy Rewind
Status: APPROVED BY OWNER, BUT CONTINGENT. This name references "Flock," which
implies affiliation with The Flock League. The owner does not yet have confirmed
permission to clip/repost the league's content (see PROFILE.md, Section C).
Owner chose to use this name now and treat getting permission as urgent.
**Do not register public social handles or post publicly under this name until
permission is confirmed.** Internal planning, drafts, and identity work under
this name are fine in the meantime.
Alternates considered: Snap Count, Fourth & Forever, Redzone Rewind, Waiver Wire,
Hail Mary.

## Niche / audience
Niche: short-form fantasy football clips/moments/reactions, sourced initially
from The Flock League ecosystem (pending permission), with room to grow into
fantasy football content generally.
Audience: sports/fantasy content browsers - casual scrollers who like sports
drama, reactions, and highlight-style moments; may not already know the league.

## Palette
Primary/dark   #12151C  near-black navy - backgrounds, clean base
Accent         #FF4B1F  hot orange-red - hype moments, callouts, thumbnails
Secondary      #F5F5F0  off-white - text on dark, clean contrast
Highlight      #FFD23F  gold-yellow - stat call-outs, "big play" moments

## Voice (writing / captions)
Tone: energetic but not screamy; confident, a little cheeky.
Use: real football/fantasy terms (snap, redzone, waiver, sleeper, bust), short
punchy sentences, present tense for drama ("he drops it").
Avoid: corporate filler, forced hype-bro slang, overly long captions.
Example line: "Waiver wire pickup goes for 3 TDs. You're welcome."
