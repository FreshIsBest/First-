# Brand: kereks_sports

Approved by the owner. This is the channel identity for the ACTIVE money-maker
(sports card content, see PROFILE.md Section C-2). Separate from `brand.md`,
which holds the paused Flock Fantasy Rewind identity - that one stays as-is in
case the owner resumes it later.

## Name / handle
Handle: kereks_sports - already exists on Instagram and TikTok, dormant about
4 years (no posts since college). No naming decision needed; accounts and
handle are already set.
Old posts: staying up. No "comeback" post or explanation of the gap - just
start posting fresh.

## Niche / audience
Niche: sports cards, mainly football with some basketball. Owner's own real
collection and real photography/video, no third-party content and no AI-
generated visuals needed for this path.
Content mix: genuine hobbyist/collector showing off his own collection, AND
flipping cards for profit. Both, not purely one or the other.
Audience: fellow card collectors/hobbyists, plus buyers for the flip side.
What the owner actually needs: production quality and growth/engagement, not
just more posts. The account has a small-but-real base to re-engage, not a
cold start.

## Palette
Owner already has a red/white/blue scheme in mind. Concrete values:
Navy      #13294B  primary - backgrounds, overlay base
Red       #C8102E  accent - price/grade/stat callouts, emphasis
Off-white #F5F5F0  text on dark, clean backgrounds
Classic sports-card red/white/blue, not tied to one specific team's exact
colors. Owner can swap exact hex values any time; these are a starting point,
not locked.

## Voice (captions)
Tone: knowledgeable and genuine; real excitement when a card earns it. Never
generic hype-bro, never corporate/salesy.
Use: real hobby language (PSA, rookie, case hit, pulled, book value, set
name), short direct captions.
Avoid: "MUST SEE!!!" clickbait, over-explaining, corporate marketing voice.
Example (PC post): "Still can't believe this one's mine. 2003 Topps Chrome
LeBron, PSA 9, been chasing it two years."
Example (flip post): "Picked this up under book. If you collect him, grab it
before it's gone."

## Visual style (content, not product)
Shooting with a phone only, no dedicated lighting/backdrop gear yet. Since
production quality is a stated priority, the practical fix is technique over
gear: natural window light or a cheap lightbox/ring light, a stable shot
(prop the phone, do not handhold), and a free editing app (CapCut is the
common pick) for consistent text overlays in the palette above. Revisit once
real clips are being made (Step 3 of 03-build-order.md, the crew).

## Platforms
Instagram and TikTok only. No YouTube, Facebook, or Pinterest for this
money-maker (unlike the blueprint's 5-platform default) - those accounts do
not exist for kereks_sports and are not part of this build.
